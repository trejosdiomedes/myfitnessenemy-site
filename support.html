Common questions

My Apple Watch workouts are not showing up.

Open More → Apps and devices and check the Health connection is on. If it is, open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → MyFitnessEnemy and make sure the categories are enabled. The app only imports today and yesterday — it will not backfill older workouts into a level.

Why did a workout score nothing?

Anything under ten minutes and under 50 calories is not counted as a session. Minutes are also weighted by intensity the way the WHO counts them, so half an hour of walking is most of a day and half an hour of running is all of it.

Why can I not change the sleep I just logged?

One night is one night. A logged night can be changed after 30 minutes, not before. A night imported from a watch can be corrected straight away.

A food cost me points.

No food is ever worth negative XP for what it is. Points are only lost when a portion takes your day past a target — calories, carbs or fat over 110% — and the cost is proportional to how far past it went.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Start over wipes everything on the device, immediately. If you made an account, More → Community → Delete account removes every row we hold on the server.

How do I cancel Coach+?

More → Try Coach+ → Manage subscription, or in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing you earned is taken away.

Reporting a problem

If something crashed or a number looks wrong, email us with what you were doing and roughly when. Settings → Export data produces a file you can attach — it is your diary in plain JSON, and it is the fastest way for us to reproduce a bug.
