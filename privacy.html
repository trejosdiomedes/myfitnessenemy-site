Privacy Policy

MyFitnessEnemy · Vestroash · Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version. Your diary — every meal, workout, night of sleep and weigh-in — is stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. If you sign in to compete with friends, what leaves your phone is a small set of numbers — a daily score, XP, a streak, and how far or how much you trained in each sport — plus your display name. We do not sell data, we do not run ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

MyFitnessEnemy is made by Vestroash. For anything in this policy, write to trejos.diomedes03@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your diary: foods and portions, workouts and their duration, sleep hours, water, body weight and body measurements.
  • Your profile: age, sex, height and weight — used to calculate your calorie and protein targets.
  • Game state: your score, XP, level, streak, medals and settings.

This is health data and we treat it as such. It lives in a single file in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, not backed up to our servers, and not readable by us.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads workouts, active energy, distance, stand hours and sleep from Apple Health so they count toward your score. The connection is one-way. The app never writes anything back to Health, and there is no setting that changes that.

You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Food search. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the words you typed or the barcode number are sent to a public food database (USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts) to look it up. Nothing about you is attached to that request.
  • Competing with others. If you create an account to use groups, challenges or the rankings, your device uploads, per day: a score, an XP total, a streak count, whether the day's training came from a connected device, and one number per sport — kilometres run, cycled, swum or walked, kilograms lifted, and minutes of HIIT, yoga or team sport. It also uploads your display name, avatar and level. Your diary is never uploaded: no meals, no body weight, no sleep, no measurements.
  • Who sees it. People in your groups see your display name and those numbers, including your records. The world rankings show your display name, avatar, level and rank-deciding number to other signed-in players. No one signed out can see any of it.
  • Call-outs and notifications. If you allow notifications while signed in, your device's push token is stored on our server so that a member of your groups can send you a call-out. A call-out is one of a fixed list of lines — nobody can type a message to you — and the record of who called out whom is kept for 30 days. Only people who share a group with you can send one. The token is deleted when you sign out, delete your account, or remove the app.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we use no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use your data to train any model.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. The app is fully usable without one; you simply compete against nobody. If you create one, we hold your display name, a user identifier, the score rows described above, and — only if you sign up with email — your email address, which is used to sign you in and reset your password and nothing else. Sign in with Apple is supported, and if you choose to hide your email we never receive it.

Service providers

Accounts, scores and notifications run on Supabase, which stores that data on our behalf and may not use it for anything else. Push notifications are delivered by Apple. There is no other third party, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

Deleting your data

  • Everything on the device: Settings → Start over. This is immediate and irreversible.
  • Your account and all server rows: on the Today screen tap ⋯ → Community → Delete my account. Every row we hold is removed immediately — scores, group memberships, call-outs and push tokens. You can also email us and we will do it for you.
  • Everything at once: deleting the app removes all local data.

How long we keep things

Local data stays until you delete it or remove the app. Server score rows stay until you delete your account. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts.

Children

MyFitnessEnemy is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold, and to object to processing. The app gives you export and delete directly; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Local data sits in the app's private container, protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption. Anything sent to our server travels over HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain, not in the app's save file.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes. The current version always lives at this address.

Not medical advice

MyFitnessEnemy is not a medical device. Its targets come from published formulae and public guidelines and are a sensible default for a healthy adult, not a prescription for you. Talk to a clinician before making significant changes to how you eat or train.
